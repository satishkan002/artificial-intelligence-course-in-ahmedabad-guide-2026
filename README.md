What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding. AI is a vast field that encompasses various subfields such as machine learning, natural language processing, and robotics. The rise of AI technologies has led to transformative changes across industries, from healthcare to finance, making it an exciting area for career growth.
